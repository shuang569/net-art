You have traveled to all corners of the world and met many people. Along the way, there have been moments when you were misunderstood, or even became a wanted criminal, and all the while, you have spent your days doing some of the most mundane jobs in the world. You fight and toil, claim minuscule rewards, then you fight some more, winning some battles and losing others as your journey goes on. And this cycle continues with no end in sight...

Even I feel bad for you, sometimes. This world has made you into a slave. So many times, you have served its will ... and not once have the powers that be ever thought to grant you the one, simple thing you wish for.

After everything I've been through, words can't describe how much I miss them, how much I want to be reunited with them.
