Your AI Slop Bores Me Human or Not Perfectly Imperfect

No Longer Human


Curated by Sabrina Huang


In an age of AI, every post on social media must be questioned for how genuine it is. Human made works (from photography to writing) has been slowly being trivialized by larger companies in favor of AI. Mixed in with beautiful photography, informative writing, complex art, etc. are generative content that is based on these original works (taken without consent). For a regular user, it's not that easy to tell if the author was human or not. In the same way users must navigate misinformation on the web, learning to traverse through the sea of AI slop is just as important. Many mainstream platforms make the usage of AI too accessible and even steal your content without permission, fueling more 'AI slop' posts. The works curated here are those that criticize AI and the current state of social media through promoting undeniable human interaction. Many of these works are recent as AI currently makes up a large portion of social media posts.
